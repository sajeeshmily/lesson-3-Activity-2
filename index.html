5 Steps to Protect Our Planet

  1. Switch off lights when not needed.
  2. Save water every day.
  3. Plant more trees.
  4. Reduce plastic use.
  5. Spread awareness about protecting Earth.

Important Environmental Terms

Climate Change
Long-term changes in Earth's temperatures and weather patterns.
Renewable Energy
Energy from sources that naturally replenish, such as sunlight and wind.
Biodiversity
The variety of living things, including plants, animals, and microorganisms.
